What we collect

  • Nothing personal by default. Names you type are converted to kanji entirely in your browser. They are not sent to our servers unless the optional pronunciation-refinement API is enabled, in which case only the name text is processed to produce katakana and is not stored.
  • Purchase state is stored in your browser's localStorage only.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Analytics (if enabled) use Plausible, a cookie-free, privacy-friendly service that collects no personal data.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no cookies for tracking, no selling of data, no ads networks.
