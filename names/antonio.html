Antonio in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Antonio is pronounced アントニオ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜刃斗仁王 — a name meaning Asia, blade (silent n), the Big Dipper, benevolence, king.
