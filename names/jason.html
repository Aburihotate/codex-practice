Jason in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Jason is pronounced ジェイソン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 樹威蒼刃 — a name meaning tree, majesty, deep blue, blade (silent n).
