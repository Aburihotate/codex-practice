Ronald in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Ronald is pronounced ロナルド in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 狼那流怒 — a name meaning wolf, graceful, flowing style, fury.
