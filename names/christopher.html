Christopher in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Christopher is pronounced クリストファー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 駆李守斗風 — a name meaning gallop, plum warrior, protector, the Big Dipper, wind (fa!).
