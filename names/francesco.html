Francesco in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Francesco is pronounced フランチェスコ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 風雷刃知守虎 — a name meaning wind, thunder, blade (silent n), knowing (approx.), protector, tiger.
