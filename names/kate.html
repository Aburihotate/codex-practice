Kate in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Kate is pronounced ケイト in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 気威斗 — a name meaning spirit, majesty, the Big Dipper.
