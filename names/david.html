David in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. David is pronounced デイビッド in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 伝威尾怒 — a name meaning legend, majesty, comet's tail, fury.
