Edward in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Edward is pronounced エドワード in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 衛怒環怒 — a name meaning guardian, fury, great ring, fury.
