Dmitri in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Dmitri is pronounced ドミトリ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 怒魅斗李 — a name meaning fury, fascination, the Big Dipper, plum warrior.
