Timothy in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Timothy is pronounced ティモシー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 帝猛獅 — a name meaning emperor, fierce, lion.
