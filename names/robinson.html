Robinson in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Robinson is pronounced ロビンソン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 狼尾刃蒼刃 — a name meaning wolf, comet's tail, blade (silent n), deep blue, blade (silent n).
