Pierre in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Pierre is pronounced ピエール in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 飛衛流 — a name meaning flight, guardian, flowing style.
