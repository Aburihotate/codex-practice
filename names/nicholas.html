Nicholas in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Nicholas is pronounced ニコラス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 仁虎雷守 — a name meaning benevolence, tiger, thunder, protector.
