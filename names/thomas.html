Thomas in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Thomas is pronounced トーマス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 斗真守 — a name meaning the Big Dipper, truth, protector.
