Anna in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Anna is pronounced アンナ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜刃那 — a name meaning Asia, blade (silent n), graceful.
