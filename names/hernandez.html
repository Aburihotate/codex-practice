Hernandez in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Hernandez is pronounced エルナンデス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 衛流那刃伝守 — a name meaning guardian, flowing style, graceful, blade (silent n), legend, protector.
