Fatima in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Fatima is pronounced ファティマ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 風帝真 — a name meaning wind (fa!), emperor, truth.
