Emily in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Emily is pronounced エミリー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 衛魅李 — a name meaning guardian, fascination, plum warrior.
