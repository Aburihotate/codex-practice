Andrew in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Andrew is pronounced アンドリュー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜刃怒龍 — a name meaning Asia, blade (silent n), fury, dragon.
