Santos in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Santos is pronounced サントス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 冴刃斗守 — a name meaning sharp brilliance, blade (silent n), the Big Dipper, protector.
