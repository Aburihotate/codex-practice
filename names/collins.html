Collins in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Collins is pronounced コリンズ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 虎李刃逗 — a name meaning tiger, plum warrior, blade (silent n), dwelling deep.
