Aisha in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Aisha is pronounced アイシャ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜威射 — a name meaning Asia, majesty, piercing shot.
