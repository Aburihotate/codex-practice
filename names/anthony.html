Anthony in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Anthony is pronounced アンソニー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜刃蒼仁 — a name meaning Asia, blade (silent n), deep blue, benevolence.
