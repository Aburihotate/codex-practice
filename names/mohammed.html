Mohammed in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Mohammed is pronounced モハメド in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 猛覇銘怒 — a name meaning fierce, supremacy, engraved mark, fury.
