Hans in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Hans is pronounced ハンス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 覇刃守 — a name meaning supremacy, blade (silent n), protector.
