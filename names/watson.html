Watson in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Watson is pronounced ワトソン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 環斗蒼刃 — a name meaning great ring, the Big Dipper, deep blue, blade (silent n).
