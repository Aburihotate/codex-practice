Brittany in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Brittany is pronounced ブリタニー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 武李太仁 — a name meaning warrior, plum warrior, grand, benevolence.
