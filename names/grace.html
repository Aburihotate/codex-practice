Grace in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Grace is pronounced グレイス in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 軍烈威守 — a name meaning army, fierce, majesty, protector.
