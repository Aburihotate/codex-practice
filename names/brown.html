Brown in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Brown is pronounced ブラウン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 武雷宇刃 — a name meaning warrior, thunder, the cosmos, blade (silent n).
