Nancy in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Nancy is pronounced ナンシー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 那刃獅 — a name meaning graceful, blade (silent n), lion.
