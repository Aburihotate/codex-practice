Carol in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Carol is pronounced キャロル in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 脚狼流 — a name meaning strong legs, wolf, flowing style.
