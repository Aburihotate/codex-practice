White in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. White is pronounced ホワイト in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 鳳環威斗 — a name meaning phoenix, great ring, majesty, the Big Dipper.
