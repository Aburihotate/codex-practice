Diego in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Diego is pronounced ディエゴ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 帝衛豪 — a name meaning emperor (voiced), guardian, mighty.
