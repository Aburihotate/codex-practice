Francois in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Francois is pronounced フランソワ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 風雷刃蒼環 — a name meaning wind, thunder, blade (silent n), deep blue, great ring.
