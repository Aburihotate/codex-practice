Jacob in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Jacob is pronounced ジェイコブ in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 樹威虎武 — a name meaning tree, majesty, tiger, warrior.
