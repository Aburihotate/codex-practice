Nguyen in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Nguyen is pronounced グエン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 軍衛刃 — a name meaning army, guardian, blade (silent n).
