Alexander in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Alexander is pronounced アレクサンダー in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜烈駆冴刃打 — a name meaning Asia, fierce, gallop, sharp brilliance, blade (silent n), striking blow.
