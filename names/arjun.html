Arjun in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Arjun is pronounced アルジュン in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 亜流獣刃 — a name meaning Asia, flowing style, wild beast, blade (silent n).
