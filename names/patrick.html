Patrick in Japanese Kanji

In Japan, foreign names are written by sound — a centuries-old art called ateji 当て字. Patrick is pronounced パトリック in Japanese, and can be written with real kanji as 覇斗李駆 — a name meaning supremacy, the Big Dipper, plum warrior, gallop.
